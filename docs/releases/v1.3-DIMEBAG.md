A hard fork that brings Midstate Walk forward, released alongside midstate's
v6.2.0 "Anselmo". From block **37,300** instead of 86,400, midwimble's proof
of work is Midstate Walk, and midstate headers follow midstate's new switch at
its block **371,500** instead of 420,000.

**Every node must upgrade before block 37,300.** It is expected between about
9 November 2026, 22:45 UTC, and 10 November, 10:40 UTC, depending on block
times, and a few hours before midstate's switch. A node on v1.2-DIMEBAG to
v1.2.2-DIMEBAG still expects the BLAKE3 chain until 86,400, so it rejects the
first Walk block. It would also reject relayed midstate headers and anchor
evidence from midstate block 371,500 on.

## What changes

- **The switch:** `WALK_ACTIVATION_HEIGHT` is 37,300. Blocks below it keep the
  BLAKE3 chain, so syncing from genesis works as before.
- **Midstate's switch is mirrored at its new height:**
  `MIDSTATE_WALK_ACTIVATION_HEIGHT` is 371,500, matching midstate v6.2.0.
- **Nothing else moves:** the Walk, both 8× resets (`WALK_TARGET_SHIFT` and
  `MIDSTATE_WALK_TARGET_SHIFT`), and v1.2.2-DIMEBAG's replacing of over-cap
  blocks are unchanged.
- **The web wallet** counts down to block 37,300, and the site and guides give
  the new heights.

## Mining

- **Merged mining pauses between the two switches:** from midwimble's block
  37,300 until midstate reaches 371,500, the merge pool mines midstate alone
  and bonded miners carry midwimble. `merge-mine` and the pool resume merging
  by themselves once both chains take the Walk.
- **Pools that merge-mine** need this release on their midwimble node and
  midstate v6.2.0 on their midstate node, before block 37,300.
- **Solo miners** need a midstate node on v6.2.0 or later too: an older one
  follows a chain the network leaves at 371,500.
